About

Algorhythm generates rhythm sight reading exercises with correct engraving: beat groupings, beaming, tuplets, ties, swing markings. Practice with the metronome, subdivisions, count-in, play along, timeline view and a tunable drone.

Turn on Tablature in Create to put a tab stave under the notation. Every note takes a pitch from the scale you choose — guitar or ukulele, all 12 keys, major, natural minor, pentatonic or blues — in open position or any of the five shapes. Guitar uses CAGED; the ukulele's four strings make the E shape impractical, so it uses CAGFD.

Turn on Grand staff mode instead for piano and keyboard: a braced treble and bass system with a proper key signature, where each hand gets its own independent rhythm and line. Pick the key, the scale and a reading level — Beginner keeps almost every note inside the staves, Advanced ranges wider onto ledger lines. Engraved with Bravura, the reference music font.

Tap the round button to play or stop. Play along lets you hear the rhythm click, the instrument, or both. Print gives clean sheets to read from.
