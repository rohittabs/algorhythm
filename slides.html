The trouble with a rhythm book

New in v3.0

It is set in Bravura now

Bravura is the reference font that professional notation software draws its symbols from, released by Steinberg under the SIL Open Font License.

It is drawn so that one staff space equals a quarter of an em, so the app scales the type to exactly four times the staff space and every clef, accidental and rest lands where an engraver would put it.

Same symbols your method book uses.

New in v3.0

Both hands, at once

Grand staff mode gives you a braced treble and bass system with a proper key signature, and a separate rhythm and a separate line for each hand.

Now it has pitch too

Switch on Tablature and a tab stave appears under the notation. Every note takes a pitch from the scale you pick, and a fretboard diagram shows the position with its roots marked.

Why most random rhythm makers are useless

Picking the notes is the easy half

The same six notes, both times. Only the second one is 6/8. A rhythm written the wrong way is a rhythm nobody can read at sight.

Odd meters are not an afterthought

One switch does the real work

Play along, on or off

On
You hear the rhythm played for you. That is learning it.
Off
You hear only the steady beat. That is reading it.

The beat never stops either way. Read it cold with the switch off, then flick it on and find out whether you were right.

With tab switched on you can pick the click, the plucked instrument, or both, and change your mind mid take.
